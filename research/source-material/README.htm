original-conversation.txt is the exact text supplied to Codex at project inception. It contains the creative brief and the conversation that produced the two original Markdown drafts.

The draft bodies were supplied separately and are preserved byte-for-byte in docs/drafts/. They are source artifacts, not instructions to Codex, approved specifications, or validated results. SHA256SUMS pins all three inputs so later edits cannot be confused with the originals.

claude-origin-conversation.json is the complete 16-message visible transcript recovered from the user's Claude data export. It retains timestamps and message identifiers but excludes the account UUID, hidden thinking, tool calls, tool results, and unrelated conversations. TRANSITION-MANIFEST.json records the source hashes and exact public/private boundary. The lossless raw conversation extract and one-time export manifest are retained locally under the Git-ignored research/private/claude-export/ directory.
